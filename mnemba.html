DISCOVER ZANZIBAR

Experience the Magic of Mnemba Island

Mnemba Island is one of the most beautiful marine destinations around Zanzibar. Its crystal-clear turquoise waters, colourful coral reefs and rich marine life make it a perfect destination for ocean lovers and adventure seekers.

With RIEU TOURS AND SAFARIS, you can enjoy an unforgettable half-day marine adventure around the Mnemba area. The experience includes a dolphin encounter, snorkeling, beautiful coral reefs, starfish and time to enjoy the tropical waters of Zanzibar.

Our professional team will make your experience comfortable, enjoyable and memorable from hotel pickup until your return.

THE EXPERIENCE

A Complete Marine Adventure

Your Mnemba adventure starts in the morning. Depending on your pickup location, our team will collect you from your hotel and take you towards the departure point.

The adventure continues with an opportunity to experience the beautiful marine environment around Mnemba. You can enjoy dolphin watching and swimming near dolphins responsibly, followed by snorkeling in clear tropical waters.

During snorkeling, discover colourful coral formations and different types of marine life. You may also encounter beautiful starfish in the shallow waters.
